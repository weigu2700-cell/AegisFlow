# AegisFlow Logo

## 设计说明

盾牌对应 Aegis 的守护含义，表达权限边界与可靠执行；盾牌内部的路径和三个节点对应 Flow，表达任务在规划、调用和校验等步骤间流转。深青与青绿色形成简洁的双色标识。

Logo 使用透明背景 PNG，README 以 280px 宽度展示。此文件为位图，不是矢量源文件。

## 生成方式

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，输出文件为 `aegisflow-logo.png`。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished minimalist standalone logo symbol for AegisFlow, an enterprise AI Agent and Skills workflow orchestration platform with human approvals, reliable execution and state recovery. Design concept: a compact geometric shield silhouette integrated with a single clear flowing path and just two or three circular workflow nodes. Make the silhouette distinctive, balanced, with generous negative space and consistent bold geometry, readable at small icon sizes. Flat vector-like graphic, crisp edges. Restrained deep teal and turquoise palette that stays legible on both white and dark gray backgrounds. One centered symbol only on a genuinely transparent background, square canvas, symbol occupies about 80 percent of canvas. No lettering, no text, no wordmark, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no mockup, no decorative border, no tiny circuit details, no robot or brain. Convey trust, controlled movement and connected execution. Original identity, not a copy of an existing brand.
